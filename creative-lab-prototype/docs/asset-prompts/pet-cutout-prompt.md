Remove ONLY the dark studio backdrop and ground, output actual transparent alpha around the sculpture. Preserve the exact original white stone long-haired cat sculpture: open eyes, face shape, ears, finely carved long fur, straight front paws, tail curled on the viewer-left, seated pose, round plinth and its engraved text GEORGE and 1974-1981. Keep original lighting, object colors, perspective, proportions and surface detail. Do not redesign or reinterpret the sculpture. No additional shadows outside the object, no background, no checkerboard baked into pixels. Include the full cat and full round base with a small transparent margin.
