## Green mark journey and closing capsule

The closing actions each use one trailing icon with a 12px text gap: Upload photo uses the upload symbol, and Browse Creations uses a right arrow.

The Hero's rectangular lime period is the start of one visual thread. As the page scrolls it rounds into the dot and moves directly to Browse Creations, then uses the page gutter to My creations, softens into a beam over the Business story, and joins the How & Why composition. A visible 22px shaded sphere sits above the masked title, then moves onto the active step rail at 14px. The step marker remains available on subsequent visits and follows manual step selection. FAQ and toolkit use quiet title markers. The travel position is determined by scroll position in both directions: upward scrolling retraces the same route. Reading sections are resting intervals: the mark is fixed to its section anchor and leaves naturally with that content. Travel is restricted to section boundaries, re-entering through the outside gutter rather than tracking every scroll movement during reading. Heading departures and arrivals share exact endpoints. The Business light stays fixed until its section starts leaving. Step 4 hands off through the whitespace below the illustration to the FAQ heading, without crossing body copy.

The fixed travel layer is decorative and pointer-transparent. It uses passive scroll/resize observation; it never changes scroll positions or adds a wheel/touch lock. Reduced motion keeps section markers and the completed composition directly visible. The existing How & Why first-visit sequence remains separate.

The closing invitation centers “Your Next Idea” and “Starts Here” above the Upload Photo / Browse Creations actions. The first e in Here keeps its normal character width while the arriving seed hits it twice: the first impact gently compresses the letter beneath a rebound, and the second shrinks it into the seed. After the second impact, growth and opening overlap into one continuous transformation around the Meshy icon. The complete arrival takes about 1.5 seconds: first contact at 360 ms, a 340 ms rebound, and a matched-size handoff that starts opening at 800 ms. The original opening spring runs at natural speed; there is no extra pause or third compression after growth. Upward scrolling reverses the handoff. Eight small lime, pale green and metallic spheres respond to cursor forces and each other. Clicking or using the keyboard closes/reopens the capsule. Mobile retains the inline letter placement and native vertical touch scrolling; Upload remains available independently of the toy.

The scene uses the installed Three.js package, with a lazy import near the footer, bounded pixel ratio, rendering suspended offscreen/when hidden, and idle rendering stopped after settling. Reduced motion renders a static open composition; WebGL failure retains the readable letter and usable CTAs. The visual/interaction inspiration is [React Bits Ballpit](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits/blob/main/src/content/Backgrounds/Ballpit/Ballpit.jsx); the small capsule and its bounded particle field are bespoke, rather than a copy of that component.
